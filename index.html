GAME HIGHLIGHTS

Built from scratch with dynamic physics, checkpoint memory, and custom cartoon art.

🌄

3 Campaign Levels

Journey through Level 1 Grasslands, Level 2 Lava Caves, and Level 3 Boss Citadel with distinct environmental mechanics and parkour traps.

🚩

3-Life Checkpoint System

Touch green flagpoles to save your progress. You get 3 lives per checkpoint—if all lives are lost, you return to the Home Screen!

🔥

Enraged Boss Battle

Battle the Citadel Boss at the end of Level 3! Stomp his head 10 times while dodging horizontal flying fireballs and jump attacks.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS

Chaddi Man runs smoothly on almost any device!

💻 Desktop / Laptop

  • • OS: Windows 10/11, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS
  • • Browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari (HTML5 Canvas support)
  • • Controls: Keyboard (A/D to move, Space/W to jump)

📱 Mobile / Tablet

  • • OS: Android, iOS / iPadOS
  • • Controls: Touch D-Pad Overlay (On-screen Left, Right, Jump buttons)
  • • Installation: Zero app store installation needed
